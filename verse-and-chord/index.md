# Verse & Chord Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-10

Verse & Chord itself collects no personal data. The only data that leaves your device is an anonymous purchase history, and only if you buy Pro.

- Your songs stay on your device, in plain text files that you control. The app never uploads them. They leave your device only when you export or share them yourself.
- There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no AI service.
- Pro purchases are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat (our purchase-validation provider). To unlock Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user id and your Apple purchase receipts, and keeps that purchase history to validate and restore your purchase. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking. See Apple's and RevenueCat's own privacy policies for how they handle that data.
- To delete your data, delete the songs in the Files app or delete the app.
- Children: the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
- Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions: see the [support page](support/).
