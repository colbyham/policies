# Verse & Chord Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-08

Verse & Chord has no account and never asks for your name, email or other personal details. A version where everything is free sends nothing off your device. A version that sells Pro sends data to one company, RevenueCat, which checks and restores Pro: an anonymous app-user id and basic device details every time the app opens, and your purchase history if you buy or restore Pro.

- Your songs stay on your device, in plain text files that you control. The app never uploads them. They leave your device only when you export or share them yourself.
- There is no account, no usage analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no AI service.
- Pro purchases are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat (our purchase-validation provider). In a version that sells Pro, the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous app-user id when it opens, with the device model, iOS and app versions, language, App Store country and Apple's per-developer device id (not the advertising id), so RevenueCat can say whether Pro is unlocked. If you buy or restore Pro, RevenueCat also receives your Apple purchase receipts and keeps that purchase history to validate and restore your purchase and to show us sales totals. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking. See Apple's and RevenueCat's own privacy policies for how they handle that data.
- To delete your data, delete the songs in the Files app or delete the app. Deleting the app does not delete RevenueCat's record of your purchases; open a support request saying so, without posting your order ID there, and we will reply with a private way to send it and have the record deleted.
- Children: the app has no account and asks no one, including children, for personal information. The only data it sends is the RevenueCat data described above, and only in a version that sells Pro.
- Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
