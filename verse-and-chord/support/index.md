# Verse & Chord Support

Need help with Verse & Chord: Lyric Notebook? Questions, bug reports and feature requests are welcome.

**Contact:** [open a support request](https://github.com/colbyham/policies/issues/new?title=Verse%20%26%20Chord%3A%20) (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version from Settings > About.

## Common questions

**Where are my songs?** Every song is a plain text file on your iPhone. Open the Files app > Browse > On My iPhone > Verse and Chord > documents. You can copy, back up or open them in any text editor.

**How do I export a song?** Open the song and tap Export. The system share sheet lets you send the text file anywhere.

**Do I need an account or internet?** No. Writing, rhymes, syllable counts and export work fully offline.

**How do I write chords?** Type the chord in brackets right before the syllable it lands on, for example `[G]Hello [D]world`.

**How do I restore a purchase?** Settings > Unlock everything > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**How do I cancel a subscription?** iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Verse & Chord. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete songs in the app or in the Files app, or delete the app. See the [privacy policy](../).
